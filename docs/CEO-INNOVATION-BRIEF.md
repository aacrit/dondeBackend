## TOP 10 "STREET TALK" FEATURES

These are the features most likely to make someone at a Chicago dinner table say: "Have you tried Donde?"

### 1. Dining Wrapped (data-storytelling-designer)
**Viral potential: EXTREME.** Spotify Wrapped for restaurants. Annual story of your dining year — top restaurants, cuisine diversity, neighborhood map, dining personality archetype. Every card is shareable on Instagram Stories.
- Why it spreads: People love sharing their identity. "I'm The Explorer" becomes a badge of pride.
- Timing: Launch December, peak sharing season.
- Cost: $0.

### 2. DondeMatch Score Reveal Animation (motion-physics-designer)
**Viral potential: HIGH.** The score counts up with spring physics, overshooting and settling. 90+ triggers golden shimmer and haptic celebration. Like watching an Apple Fitness ring close.
- Why it spreads: The "moment of reveal" creates anticipation. Screen recordings will spread.
- Cost: $0. Pure CSS + JS.

### 3. The L-Line Discovery Mode (spatial-map-innovator)
**Viral potential: EXTREME.** Swipe along CTA L lines, see restaurants clustered by station. "Get off here" for the best food stops. The most Chicago feature any app has ever built.
- Why it spreads: Every Chicagoan rides the L. No other app treats the transit system as a discovery mechanism.
- Cost: $0. CTA station data is public.

### 4. Chicago Cuisine Passport (gamification-engagement-designer)
**Viral potential: HIGH.** A visual passport with cultural stamps for each of Chicago's 30 cuisine types. Fill your passport, earn "Chicago Global Citizen." Shareable as image.
- Why it spreads: Collection mechanic + cultural celebration + shareable progress.
- Cost: $0.

### 5. Conversational Search (voice-conversational-designer)
**Viral potential: HIGH.** Type or speak naturally: "cozy Italian in Lincoln Park for a date, not too expensive." One sentence replaces 5 form fields. Donde understands everything.
- Why it spreads: The "it just gets me" moment. People will demo this to friends.
- Cost: $0 for text. Voice via free Web Speech API.

### 6. Taste DNA / Taste Blend for Groups (personalization-ai-architect + social-community-designer)
**Viral potential: EXTREME.** Multi-dimensional taste fingerprint + Spotify Blend for dining. Input your group, get restaurants that satisfy everyone. Shareable archetype cards.
- Why it spreads: Group dining is Chicago's #1 social activity. "Let's Donde it" becomes a verb.
- Cost: $0.

### 7. Gesture-Driven Restaurant Dismissal (motion-physics-designer)
**Viral potential: MEDIUM-HIGH.** Swipe cards left to dismiss, right to save. Physics-based — card follows finger with friction, rotation, momentum. Like Tinder for restaurants.
- Why it spreads: Immediately familiar interaction pattern. Satisfying physics make it addictive.
- Cost: $0.

### 8. Pull-to-Discover (motion-physics-designer)
**Viral potential: MEDIUM-HIGH.** Pull down on results with rubber-band physics to get a new recommendation. Logo morphs into loading spinner. New result bounces in.
- Why it spreads: The "one more" mechanic. Slot machine psychology applied to discovery.
- Cost: $0.

### 9. Share Cards (social-community-designer)
**Viral potential: EXTREME.** One-tap beautiful restaurant share cards — photo, DondeMatch score, cultural theme styling. Perfect for Instagram Stories, iMessage, WhatsApp.
- Why it spreads: Every share is free advertising. Cultural theme styling makes every card unique and beautiful.
- Cost: $0.

### 10. Easter Eggs (micro-interaction-designer)
**Viral potential: HIGH.** Hidden surprises — search "Chicago" and see the flag. Search at 2am and get "Still hungry? Same." Snow particles during actual Chicago snowstorms. Cubs colors near Wrigley on game day.
- Why it spreads: Discovery and sharing of hidden features creates community moments. "Did you know if you..."
- Cost: $0.

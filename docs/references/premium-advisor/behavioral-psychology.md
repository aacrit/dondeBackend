# DondeAI Behavioral Psychology & Retention Playbook

## The Hook Model Applied to Restaurant Discovery

### 1. TRIGGER — Getting Users to Open the App

**External Triggers (you control these):**

| Trigger | Timing | Copy Pattern | Psychology |
|---------|--------|-------------|------------|
| Dinner planning | 4-5 PM weekdays | "What's for dinner tonight? 🍽️" | Routine anchor |
| Weekend discovery | Sat 11 AM | "New spot alert in [neighborhood]" | Novelty seeking |
| Weather-based | Rainy evenings | "Cozy dinner weather — here's a perfect pick" | Situational relevance |
| Post-visit | 24h after dining | "How was [restaurant]? Rate to improve your picks" | Recency + investment |
| Streak reminder | 6 PM if streak at risk | "Your discovery streak is at risk! 🔥" | Loss aversion |
| Social | When friend uses app | "[Friend] just discovered a hidden gem near you" | Social proof |

**Internal Triggers (you build these over time):**
- "I'm hungry" → open DondeAI (replaces "open Yelp/Google")
- "I'm bored with my usual spots" → open DondeAI
- "We need a place for [occasion]" → open DondeAI
- "I wonder what's good in this neighborhood" → open DondeAI

**How internal triggers form:** Repeated external triggers that consistently deliver value. After ~5 successful recommendations, the external trigger becomes unnecessary.

### 2. ACTION — The Simplest Possible Interaction

**Reduce friction to near-zero:**

Current: Open app → Navigate → Type query → Wait → See results
Target: Open app → See smart suggestion OR tap mood chip → See recommendation

**Friction-reducing features:**
- **Smart home screen:** Pre-populated suggestion based on time, location, history
- **Mood chips:** "Quick bite", "Date night", "Adventurous", "Comfort food" — one tap
- **Voice input:** "Find me something spicy near Wicker Park"
- **Recent moods:** "Last time you wanted Italian comfort food — try again?"
- **Skip the keyboard:** Most interactions should be tappable, not typed

**Fogg Behavior Model:** Behavior = Motivation × Ability × Trigger
- Motivation: They're hungry (high motivation)
- Ability: Must be EASY (reduce to one tap)
- Trigger: Your push notification at the right moment

### 3. VARIABLE REWARD — The Addictive Part

**Why DondeAI has a natural advantage:** Every restaurant recommendation is inherently variable. Unlike Spotify (same song plays the same), each restaurant visit is unique. Lean into this.

**Reward types to implement:**

**Rewards of the Hunt (searching and finding):**
- Each recommendation reveals a new restaurant — the "what will it be?" anticipation
- "Hidden Gems" — restaurants with <50 reviews but 90+ Donde Score
- Weekly "Donde Discovery" — a curated pick from a cuisine you haven't tried
- Neighborhood unlock: first time exploring a new area

**Rewards of the Tribe (social connection):**
- "Trending in your circle" — what friends are eating
- "Chicago's #1 pick this week" — city-wide social proof
- Share your Donde Score card on Instagram (designed to be beautiful)
- "You and [friend] both love [cuisine]" — taste matching

**Rewards of the Self (mastery and completion):**
- "Cuisine Passport" filling up as you try new cuisines
- "Chicago Explorer" map showing neighborhoods visited
- Personal taste profile becoming more refined over time
- "Your recommendations are getting 23% more accurate"

### 4. INVESTMENT — Creating Switching Costs

**Every investment makes the product better AND harder to leave:**

| Investment | What User Does | What App Gets | Switching Cost |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| Rate a restaurant | 1-5 stars + optional note | Taste profile data | Personalization loss |
| Save a favorite | Tap heart icon | Preference signal | Curated list loss |
| Set dietary prefs | One-time setup | Filter accuracy | Setup effort loss |
| Build cuisine passport | Visit new cuisines | Engagement data | Progress loss |
| Share a recommendation | Social post | Viral distribution | Social identity |
| Write a micro-review | 1-2 sentence take | Content + taste signal | Content loss |

**The key insight:** Each investment should directly improve the next recommendation. Make this visible: "Based on your 12 ratings, your next pick will be even better."

## Gamification Strategy (Premium, Not Gimmicky)

### DO: Exploration-Themed Progress

DondeAI's gamification should feel like a culinary adventure, not a video game.

**Chicago Explorer Map:**
- 77 community areas to discover
- Neighborhoods light up when you get a recommendation there
- "Undiscovered" neighborhoods glow with mystery
- Monthly goal: "Explore 3 new neighborhoods this month"
- Visual: stylized map of Chicago that fills with cultural theme colors

**Cuisine Passport:**
- 15 cultural themes = 15 passport stamps
- Each stamp requires trying a restaurant from that cuisine
- Unlocking a stamp reveals that theme's unique particle animation
- "Complete your passport" creates a natural 15-visit retention loop
- Shareable: "I've explored 11/15 cuisines on DondeAI"

**Discovery Streaks:**
- Track weekly "new restaurant" streaks
- 3-week streak: "Rising Explorer" badge
- 8-week streak: "Chicago Connoisseur" status
- Streak Freeze: purchasable or earnable (1 free per month)
- Streak at risk notification at 6 PM on the last day

### DON'T: Cheap Gamification

- ❌ Points/coins/currency (feels transactional for dining)
- ❌ Competitive leaderboards (dining is personal)
- ❌ Generic badges ("First Review!" — boring)
- ❌ Daily login rewards (feels desperate)
- ❌ Countdown timers on deals (signals discount app, not premium)

## Onboarding Psychology

### The 3-Screen Rule

Research shows each additional onboarding screen drops completion by ~15%.

**Screen 1: Identity** (2 seconds)
- Beautiful cultural theme animation auto-playing
- "Discover Chicago, one perfect restaurant at a time"
- [Get Started] button

**Screen 2: Taste** (5 seconds)
- "What sounds good right now?" — 6 mood chips
- Tapping a chip immediately shows a preview recommendation
- This IS the aha moment — they see the product working

**Screen 3: Commit** (3 seconds)
- "Want picks tailored to you?" 
- [Sign up with Apple/Google] — single tap SSO
- "Skip for now" always visible (reduces anxiety)

**What NOT to do:**
- ❌ Ask for name, email, password on separate screens
- ❌ Show a tutorial carousel (nobody reads them)
- ❌ Ask for location permission before showing value
- ❌ Require account creation before the first recommendation
- ❌ Show a loading spinner during onboarding (preload everything)

### Permission Priming

Never show iOS permission dialogs cold. Always prime first:

**Location:**
"DondeAI finds restaurants near you. Allow location access?"
[Custom screen with map illustration] → [Allow] → iOS dialog

**Notifications:**
After first successful recommendation:
"Want a heads-up when we find your next perfect spot?"
[Custom screen showing example notification] → [Sure!] → iOS dialog

**This doubles opt-in rates** because users understand the value before the system dialog appears.

## Pricing Psychology (When Ready to Monetize)

### Freemium Tier Design

**Free tier should be generous enough to build the habit:**
- 5 recommendations per week
- Access to all 15 cultural themes  
- Basic taste profile
- "Powered by DondeAI" on share cards

**Premium tier ($4.99/mo or $39.99/yr):**
- Unlimited recommendations
- "Hidden Gems" access (exclusive low-review, high-score spots)
- Advanced taste profile with cuisine breakdown
- No branding on share cards
- Priority during peak hours (Friday/Saturday dinner)
- Streak Freeze inventory (3/month vs. 1)

### Pricing Psychology Tactics

- **Anchoring:** Show yearly price first ($39.99), then monthly ($4.99) feels cheap
- **Per-day framing:** "$0.11 per day — less than a tip"
- **Loss framing after trial:** "You'll lose your taste profile and 3-week streak"
- **Social proof:** "Join 12,000+ Chicago food lovers"
- **Decoy effect:** If adding a middle tier, make it obviously worse than premium

## Metrics to Track

### North Star Metric
**Weekly Active Recommendations Used** — not DAU or sessions

### Retention Funnel
1. D1 retention: Did they come back the next day? (Target: 40%)
2. D7 retention: Did they come back within a week? (Target: 25%)
3. D30 retention: Monthly active? (Target: 15%)
4. Recommendation-to-visit rate: Did they actually go? (Target: 30%)

### Hook Model Health
- Trigger → Action time: <3 seconds (notification to recommendation)
- Actions per session: 1.5+ (at least one recommendation + one rating)
- Investment rate: 40%+ of users rate after visiting
- Streak participation: 20%+ of MAU maintaining a streak

### Premium Conversion
- Free-to-trial: 8-12%
- Trial-to-paid: 40-50%
- Monthly churn: <5%
